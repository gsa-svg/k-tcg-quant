Free One Piece TCG data — grading population and auction results

Three CSVs, regenerated daily, free under CC BY 4.0. They cover grading population split by grader and printing, completed eBay auction outcomes, and Japanese booster-box prices. Auction rows record settled outcomes rather than active asking prices.

1. Grading population — opbox-grading-population.csv

One row per set per printing. Japanese and English are different print runs with different pull rates, so we record them separately and never add them together — a combined gem rate describes neither. The same applies across graders: a PSA 10, a CGC Pristine 10 and a TAG 10 are different standards, so the columns sit side by side and are never summed.

set_code · edition — japanese or english
psa_total · psa10_rate_pct · psa_weekly_add — cumulative PSA count, PSA 10 share, and how many were added in the latest week we recorded
cgc_total · cgc_pristine10 · cgc_gem_mint10 — CGC splits its top grade in two; Pristine 10 is the stricter one
tag_total · tag_10 · tag_10p — TAG's 10 and its stricter 10P
as_of — the date that row was collected. Blank means we have not confirmed a figure, not zero.

2. Auction results by day — opbox-auction-daily.csv

Every row is one day and one product type. The same data is explained and charted on the auction results page. Each auction is read after it closed, so median_price_usd is the final winning bid, not a mid-auction figure — the difference is real, since sniping regularly moves a price in the last minutes. Auctions that ended unsold stay in the data as the denominator of sell_through_pct; dropping them would flatter every price.

date · kind — all, box, card or pack
auctions · sold · sell_through_pct — how many ran, how many actually sold
median_price_usd — median final winning bid, per item for multi-item lots
median_bids — median bid count, a rough read on competition

3. Japanese booster box prices — opbox-set-prices.csv

Current sealed box price per set in KRW and USD, with change measured from each set's tracking start date — not from its release date, except where launch_tracked is true. Reprint history moved to the individual set guides, where it can be shown with its sources.

How it's built

Prices are aggregates of real completed sales and verified active listings, normalised to one sealed Japanese booster box. Grading counts come from each grader's own public population reporting, collected weekly and appended to a ledger we never rewrite. Auction figures are read from listings after they close. We publish derived aggregates only — never raw listing dumps. Where a value cannot be verified we leave it blank rather than estimate it.
