How to use this data · where the rest of the site is

Compare an auction median with recent fixed-price sales and current asking prices; none is a complete market on its own. A large gap is a reason to check sample size, exact variant, condition, shipping and closing time before drawing a conclusion. On the latest full day (2026-08-29) we tracked 557 auctions ending, of which 161 sold.
