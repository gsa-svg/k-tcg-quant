OP-05 vs OP-06: tracked booster-box price and PSA data

Research and data: OP Box Index · Published July 4, 2026 · Updated August 10, 2026 · Data sources & methodology

OP-05 Awakening of the New Era (released 2023-12-08) and OP-06 Wings of the Captain (released 2024-03-15) have different entry prices, sale samples and chase-card distributions. This comparison records those differences without turning one dated snapshot into a buying verdict.

OP-05 Awakening of the New Era

  • Earlier release gives it a longer secondary-market history than OP-06.
  • Chase-card strength is concentrated around multiple Luffy alternate-art and special-art pulls plus a 1st Anniversary print.
  • Being an earlier set, verified sold history tends to run deeper, which makes price signals more reliable.

OP-06 Wings of the Captain

  • Newer set with a shorter post-release history, so price trends can still be more volatile.
  • Its headline chase card is a Treasure Rare Nami alongside strong Zoro alternate-art and SEC pulls.
  • Its smaller recorded PSA population may reflect less time in market, submission demand or both; it is not an opening count.

What the dated snapshot shows

OP-05 is roughly 1.8× the price of OP-06 on the Japanese side ($211 vs $115 sold). In the English sample, OP-05 is $1,005 against OP-06's $269, a 3.7× difference. The table establishes the gap, but public data does not isolate print volume, reprint timing or buyer demand as its cause.

OP-05's cheapest verified Japanese listing is $151.89 — about 28% below its $211 sold median, while OP-06's cheapest listing at $192.38 sits 67% above its $115 sold median. That mismatch is a reason to inspect listing condition, seller location, shipping and sale dates rather than assume either figure is automatically the current transaction price.

PSA records 68,854 OP-05 grades versus 36,818 OP-06 grades. The difference can reflect time in market, submission selection and collector demand. It does not reveal how many boxes were opened. Gem rate is also sample-specific: 88.9% for OP-05 and 90.6% for OP-06 among the recorded submissions. See the limits in our population methodology.

Sample-size caveat: OP-06's Japanese sold median rests on only 12 completed sales versus 25 for OP-05. One unusual transaction therefore has more influence on OP-06. The English samples contain 102 and 119 sales.

How to compare them properly

  • Check the completed-sale sample and the exact active listing separately; the two can differ sharply.
  • Use PSA gem rate and population as grading-market context, not as proof of sealed supply.
  • Recheck current data on the set pages because every number above is dated.
