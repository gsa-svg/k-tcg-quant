OP-16 booster box price, one month after launch: what the daily data shows

Research and data: OP Box Index · Published July 17, 2026 · Updated August 10, 2026 · Data sources & methodology

OP-16 "The Time of Battle" launched in Japan on May 30, 2026 and in English on June 12, a 13-day gap. We tracked the Japanese sealed box price from the pre-release phase through July 16. This report records that path and separates it from the later grading data.

Early grading sample

PSA's population report tracks submissions, not boxes opened in the same week. One month in, only 82 OP-16 cards had been graded all-time: 10 new grades in the week of June 24, 13 the next week, then 38 in the week of July 8. The early gem rate was about 97%, but this selected sample was too small to support a print-quality conclusion. Weekly bars update on the OP-16 page.

JP vs EN: the premium almost disappeared

The English OP-16 box traded near $188 against the Japanese box at $150 — a premium of +25%, the smallest ratio in the dated sample. The release gap was also the shortest, but the data does not establish it as the sole cause. Full table in the Japanese vs English data report.

Recorded result: the July 16 value was $150, 32% below the April pre-release level and 22% above the June 15 low. Later PSA additions do not prove current box openings or remaining sealed supply. Current figures are on the live OP-16 page.

FAQ

Is an OP-16 box worth buying now?

This report cannot answer that from a July snapshot. It records $220 pre-release, a $123 post-launch low and $150 on July 16. Check the current set page, completed-sale sample, seller and seal before comparing an offer.

Will OP-16 get a restock?

Bandai has not announced an OP-16 reprint as of July 17, 2026. We do not publish rumors; if an official reprint is announced, it will show up in our weekly report and this page will be updated.

Should I wait for OP-17 instead?

They are different sets with different prices and card lists. The OP-17 data page places its pre-release quote beside the recorded launch paths for OP-14 through OP-16 without predicting the next move.
