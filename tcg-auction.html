How many card auctions end each day

2026-08-07–2026-09-02

⚠ This is not market size. The number of auctions each game ends per day differs enormously (37,980 for Pokemon vs 285 for Riftbound), yet we settle roughly the same couple of hundred per game per day. Our share of each game therefore ranges from a fraction of a percent to well over half, and a hammer-value total tracks how much we saw, not how big the market is. For comparison across games, use sell-through or median winning bid.
