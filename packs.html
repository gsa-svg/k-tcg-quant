One Piece booster box prices — common questions
How much is a One Piece booster box?

Sealed Japanese One Piece booster boxes currently range from about $80 (OP-07 500 Years in the Future and OP-08 Two Legends) to $299 (OP-01 Romance Dawn) — median of completed eBay sales, as of 2026-10-23.

Which One Piece booster box is the most valuable?

OP-01 Romance Dawn is the most expensive sealed Japanese box we track at about $299, ahead of OP-05 Awakening of the New Era at $175.

Are One Piece booster boxes going up or down in price?

Of the 18 Japanese sets with a 4-week reference point, 7 are up and 7 are down over the last 4 weeks (latest weekly sold median vs 4 weeks earlier); the largest gain is OP-12 at +4.8% and the largest fall is OP-17 at -9.1%.
