Set notes — price context, reprints, factory-seal check, background

Blackbeard, twice

Legacy of the Master hands its top end to the game's great villain: Marshall D. Teach appears twice at the summit, in gold-stamped and silver-stamped SP treatments — a two-tier pairing that stands out even in a run full of variant experiments. The manga-art Jewelry Bonney behind them is the kind of left-field third chase that quietly builds a following.

Villain-led boxes are rarer than protagonist-led ones and their buyers skew collector over player. Watch how the two Teach variants price against each other in the table — that gold-versus-silver gap is a small market signal no other set currently offers.

OP-12 box price: the tracked trajectory

The Japanese Legacy of the Master sealed box entered our tracking in May 2026 around $75 and stands near $110 as of 2026-10-01 — it gained 47% over the window, peaking at $110 in the week of 2026-09-24. The English-language Legacy of the Master box trades near $274 as of 2026-10-06 (+12% over its tracked window) — about 2.5x the Japanese box (JP as of 2026-10-01, EN as of 2026-10-06).
