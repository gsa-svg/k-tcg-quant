Set notes — price context, reprints, factory-seal check, background

Zoro's turn, Robin's throwback

Wings of the Captain hands the flagship spot to the manga-art Roronoa Zoro and backs it with one of the run's best character ideas: Nico Robin printed under her old Baroque Works identity, Ms. All Sunday, in SP treatment. A treasure-rare Nami completes the podium.

Three different card classes at the top — manga, SP, treasure rare — spread this box's value across more than one card, the opposite of its predecessor's one-card economy. That balance is why OP-06 keeps coming up as a first Japanese box for collectors crossing over from the English game.

OP-06 box price: the tracked trajectory

The Japanese Wings of the Captain sealed box entered our tracking on 2026-07-08 around $142 and stands near $159 as of 2026-09-23 — it gained 12% over the window, peaking at $170 in the week of 2026-08-26. The English-language Wings of the Captain box trades near $380 as of 2026-10-14 (+11% over its tracked window) — about 2.4x the Japanese box (JP as of 2026-09-23, EN as of 2026-10-14). This ratio does not explain why.

How the OP-06 box price compares with its tracked range (2026-10-15)

The box trades at $159, 6% under its tracked peak of $170 and up 12% over our tracking window.
