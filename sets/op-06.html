Set notes — price context, reprints, factory-seal check, background

Zoro's turn, Robin's throwback

Wings of the Captain hands the flagship spot to the manga-art Roronoa Zoro and backs it with one of the run's best character ideas: Nico Robin printed under her old Baroque Works identity, Ms. All Sunday, in SP treatment. A treasure-rare Nami completes the podium.

Three different card classes at the top — manga, SP, treasure rare — spread this box's value across more than one card, the opposite of its predecessor's one-card economy. That balance is why OP-06 keeps coming up as a first Japanese box for collectors crossing over from the English game.

OP-06 box price: the six-month trajectory

The Japanese Wings of the Captain sealed box entered Jan 2026 around $73 and stands near $99 as of 2026-07-12 — it gained 36% over the window, peaking at $121 in the week of 2026-07-08. The English-language Wings of the Captain box trades near $384 over the same period (+39% since January) — about 3.9x the Japanese box. This ratio does not explain why.

The box trades at $99, 18% under its tracked peak of $121 and up 36% over our tracking window. The top card alone (Roronoa Zoro Alternate Art Manga) is worth about 25x the box, so the visible chase value is highly concentrated. Official pull rates are unpublished. It is not opening expected value.
