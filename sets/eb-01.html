The extra-booster formula, first try

Memorial Collection introduced the extra-booster format to the Japanese run: a smaller checklist, heavy on reprints and celebration cards, sold as a look back at the game's opening stretch. Its market surprise was Tony Tony Chopper — the manga-art alternate became the face of the box — with a Mr. 2 Bon Kurei alternate as the run's best fan-service pick.

Extra boosters live or die on their alternates, because the base cards are known quantities. EB-01's alternates aged well, which is why this nominally minor product keeps a permanent place in our daily tracking alongside the main sets.

EB-01 box price: the six-month trajectory

The Japanese sealed box entered Jan 2026 around $90 and stands near $116 as of 2026-07-11 — it gained 29% over the window, peaking at $125 in the week of 2026-06-24. The English-language EB-01 box trades near $859 over the same period (+4% since January) — about 7.4x the Japanese box, a gap driven by print volume and Western demand rather than card content.

Is an EB-01 booster box worth buying? (Aug 2026)

The box sits near its tracked high ($116 vs peak $125, +29% over our window) — the market has rewarded holders, and a buyer today is paying for that momentum to continue. The chase math is lottery-shaped: the top card alone (Tony TonyChopper Alternate Art Manga) is worth about 18x the box, so a box's expected value concentrates in a few low-odds hits.
