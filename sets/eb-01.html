Set notes — price context, reprints, factory-seal check, background

The extra-booster formula, first try

Memorial Collection introduced the extra-booster format to the Japanese run: a smaller checklist, heavy on reprints and celebration cards, sold as a look back at the game's opening stretch. Its market surprise was Tony Tony Chopper — the manga-art alternate became the face of the box — with a Mr. 2 Bon Kurei alternate as the run's best fan-service pick.

Extra boosters live or die on their alternates, because the base cards are known quantities. EB-01's alternates aged well, which is why this nominally minor product keeps a permanent place in our daily tracking alongside the main sets.

EB-01 box price: the tracked trajectory

The Japanese Memorial Collection sealed box entered our tracking in Jul 2026 around $110 and stands near $135 as of 2026-08-19 — it gained 23% over the window, peaking at $135 in the week of 2026-08-05.
