Set notes — price context, reprints, factory-seal check, background

PRB-01 box price, reprint evidence and factory seal

As of 2026-09-01, current eBay asks center near $215 across 13 verified listings. Completed eBay sales last put the box near $159 on 2026-08-31. PRB-01 is a 10-pack premium reprint product rather than a 24-pack main booster. Its chase demand is spread across stamped versions of established characters, so comparisons should use PRB-01 listings only and should not mix in the original printings.

Reprint record: The source ledger contains a MediaWorld reprint listing, but the page does not provide a distinct restock date or print quantity. It confirms a documented reprint record, not the scale of supply or a future reprint schedule.

Factory-seal check: A Japanese PRB-01 box should retain its original Bandai security tape uncut. Confirm the 10-pack product format, top tape, side seams, bottom and product code; exclude loose packs and mixed PRB-01 lots.

The best-of box

Premium Booster is a different kind of product from a numbered set: a curated reprint box whose chase list is older hits wearing new stamps. Our table for it is led by a Luffy carrying its original OP-05 119 number, a Chopper with an EB-01 stamp, and a Nami from OP-01 — the run's greatest-hits album.

In Japan, premium boosters have moved through narrower channels than open retail, which gives PRB products a supply rhythm unlike the main sets: tight at release, loosening in waves. Read its price history against that cadence, not against a numbered set's.
