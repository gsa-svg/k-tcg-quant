Set notes — price context, reprints, factory-seal check, background

The anniversary box

Anime 25th Collection is the celebration box, built for the anime's quarter-century with art direction that leans nostalgic. A Boa Hancock SP leads it, the manga-art Luffy 061 sits close behind, and a Chopper SP holds the mascot slot.

Anniversary products pull in buyers who otherwise never touch the card game, and that widened base shows in how often this box surfaces in our deals module. The long-term question is the usual one for celebration sets — whether demand outlives the birthday — and the daily numbers on this page are how we are finding out.

EB-02 box price: the tracked trajectory

The Japanese Anime 25th Collection sealed box entered our tracking in Jul 2026 around $148 and stands near $135 as of 2026-10-01 — it fell 9% over the window, peaking at $150 in the week of 2026-07-16. The English-language Anime 25th Collection box trades near $808 as of 2026-10-02 (+27% over its tracked window) — about 6.0x the Japanese box (JP as of 2026-10-01, EN as of 2026-10-02). This ratio does not explain why.

How the EB-02 box price compares with its tracked range (Oct 2026)

The box trades at $135, 10% under its tracked peak of $150 and down 9% over our tracking window. The top 2 cards (Boa Hancock SP and Monkey D. Luffy 010 Alternate Art) are each worth about 4.7x the box. Official pull rates are unpublished. It is not opening expected value.
