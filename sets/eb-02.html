Set notes — price context, reprints, factory-seal check, background

The anniversary box

Anime 25th Collection is the celebration box, built for the anime's quarter-century with art direction that leans nostalgic. A Boa Hancock SP leads it, the manga-art Luffy 061 sits close behind, and a Chopper SP holds the mascot slot.

Anniversary products pull in buyers who otherwise never touch the card game, and that widened base shows in how often this box surfaces in our deals module. The long-term question is the usual one for celebration sets — whether demand outlives the birthday — and the daily numbers on this page are how we are finding out.

Reprints & original price

  • Original Japanese release: 2025-01-25 (Bandai official).
  • Original Japanese MSRP: ¥5,280 per 24-pack box (about $34).
  • No reprint or restock record in our source ledger.
