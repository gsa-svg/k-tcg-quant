Set notes — price context, reprints, factory-seal check, background

Marineford, and the manga rare that defined a card class

Paramount War puts the Marineford war on cardboard, and one card from it changed how the whole game is collected: the manga-art parallel of Portgas D. Ace. That card made the comic-panel treatment a chase class in its own right — every later set's manga rare gets compared to it. Alternate-art Sanji and Garp fill out the top of the box.

As the second main set its supply story is nearly as tight as OP-01's. When Ace moves, the sealed box tends to follow; few boxes in the run are as single-card-driven as this one, and the graded population of that Ace is the number that decides whether the premium holds.
