Set notes — price context, reprints, factory-seal check, background

Three admirals, one cycle

The Time of Battle is the newest main set in the tracker, and its top three form something the run had not produced before in our table: a complete themed cycle. Manga-art Sakazuki, Kuzan and Borsalino — the three original admirals — sit together at the summit, and cycle chases invite completionism: plenty of buyers here want all three or none.

A box this fresh moves on opening-volume news more than anything durable. Our sell-through and auction figures for OP-16 will mean far more by autumn than they do today; the honest read is "watch", and this page refreshes daily while the picture forms.

OP-16 box price: the six-month trajectory

The Japanese The Time of Battle sealed box entered Apr 2026 around $220 and stands near $122 as of 2026-08-19 — it fell 45% over the window, peaking at $220 in the week of 2026-04-27. The English-language The Time of Battle box trades near $188 over the same period (-53% since January) — about 1.5x the Japanese box. This ratio does not explain why.

How the OP-16 box price compares with its tracked range (Aug 2026)

The current value is about 45% below the tracked peak ($245 → $136). Past peaks do not set future floors. The top card alone (Sakazuki (Manga)) is worth about 23x the box, so the visible chase value is highly concentrated. Official pull rates are unpublished. It is not opening expected value.

Reprints & original price

  • Original Japanese release: 2026-05-30 (Bandai official).
  • Original Japanese MSRP: ¥5,280 per 24-pack box (about $33).
  • Post-release lottery sale: May 2026, Jun 2026 — a Premium Bandai / Bandai Namco shop draw held after release. Bandai does not call these reprints, so we do not either.

How fast is the OP-16 PSA population growing?

For The Time of Battle, between 2026-06-24 and 2026-08-12, the recorded PSA population increased by 1,594 grades — peaking at 531 in the week of 2026-08-05, with the latest week at 237 (cooling off from its peak). The all-time set total is 1,965. Grades are not a box-opening count.
