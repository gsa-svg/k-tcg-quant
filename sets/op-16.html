Set notes — price context, reprints, factory-seal check, background

Three admirals, one cycle

The Time of Battle is the newest main set in the tracker, and its top three form something the run had not produced before in our table: a complete themed cycle. Manga-art Sakazuki, Kuzan and Borsalino — the three original admirals — sit together at the summit, and cycle chases invite completionism: plenty of buyers here want all three or none.

A box this fresh moves on opening-volume news more than anything durable. Our sell-through and auction figures for OP-16 will mean far more by autumn than they do today; the honest read is "watch", and this page refreshes daily while the picture forms.

OP-16 box price: the tracked trajectory

The Japanese The Time of Battle sealed box entered our tracking on 2026-06-09 around $146 and stands near $97 as of 2026-10-13 — it fell 34% over the window, peaking at $147 in the week of 2026-06-16. The English-language The Time of Battle box trades near $233 as of 2026-10-13 (+0% over its tracked window) — about 2.4x the Japanese box. This ratio does not explain why.

How the OP-16 box price compares with its tracked range (2026-10-13)

The current value is about 34% below the tracked peak ($147 → $97). Past peaks do not set future floors. The top 3 cards (Kuzan (Manga), Sakazuki (Manga) and Borsalino (Manga)) are each worth about 9.6x the box. Official pull rates are unpublished. It is not opening expected value.

Reprints & original price

  • Original Japanese release: 2026-05-30 (Bandai official).

  • Post-release lottery sale: 2026-05, 2026-06 — a Premium Bandai / Bandai Namco shop draw held after release. Bandai does not call these reprints, so we do not either.
