Set notes — price context, reprints, factory-seal check, background

Greatest hits, volume two

The second Premium Booster repeats the formula with a different tracklist: its top end carries a Sanji manga-art with its original OP-06 119 number, the Gum-Gum Giant alternate art, and a stamped SP Luffy from EB-02. Selection boxes like this are effectively Bandai's editorial statement about which cards mattered.

Because every chase here is a restamped known quantity, PRB-02 prices lean on their originals: the stamped copy usually trades under the original print, and the gap between the two is a useful scarcity signal. Both versions sit in our per-card tracker — compare them before paying either price.
