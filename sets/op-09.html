Set notes — price context, reprints, factory-seal check, background

One card: Roger

Emperors in the New World gathers the yonko era into one box, and the market read it instantly: the Gol D. Roger chase is the card, with the manga-art Luffy 119 and a manga Shanks forming the second tier. A Pirate King card topping a set about emperors is as clean as set design gets.

In our table this set's value curve is steep — a big step from first place down to the rest. Steep-curve boxes reward buyers who care about the one hit and punish those who valued the median pull, which is worth knowing before comparing it against flatter sets like OP-03.

OP-09 box price: the tracked trajectory

The Japanese Emperors in the New World sealed box entered our tracking in May 2026 around $99 and stands near $128 as of 2026-08-21 — it gained 29% over the window, peaking at $144 in the week of 2026-06-26. The English-language Emperors in the New World box trades near $639 as of 2026-08-24 (+30% over its tracked window) — about 5.0x the Japanese box (JP as of 2026-08-21, EN as of 2026-08-24). This ratio does not explain why.

How the OP-09 box price compares with its tracked range (Aug 2026)

The box trades at $128, 11% under its tracked peak of $144 and up 29% over our tracking window. The top card alone (Gol D. Roger) is worth about 39x the box, so the visible chase value is highly concentrated. Official pull rates are unpublished. It is not opening expected value.

Reprints & original price

  • Original Japanese release: 2024-08-31 (Bandai official).
  • Original Japanese MSRP: ¥5,280 per 24-pack box (about $33).
  • Post-release lottery sale: Oct 2024 — a Premium Bandai / Bandai Namco shop draw held after release. Bandai does not call these reprints, so we do not either.

How fast is the OP-09 PSA population growing?
