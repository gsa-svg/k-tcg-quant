Set notes — price context, reprints, factory-seal check, background

One card: Roger

Emperors in the New World gathers the yonko era into one box, and the market read it instantly: the Gol D. Roger chase is the card, with the manga-art Luffy 119 and a manga Shanks forming the second tier. A Pirate King card topping a set about emperors is as clean as set design gets.

In our table this set's value curve is steep — a big step from first place down to the rest. Steep-curve boxes reward buyers who care about the one hit and punish those who valued the median pull, which is worth knowing before comparing it against flatter sets like OP-03.

The Japanese Emperors in the New World sealed box entered Jan 2026 around $74 and stands near $114 as of 2026-07-12 — it gained 54% over the window, peaking at $134 in the week of 2026-06-03.
