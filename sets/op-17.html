Reprint record: One pre-release Premium Bandai lottery is on record (2026-07, entries before the 2026-08-22 release). That is advance allocation of the initial print run, not extra supply, and no post-release restock has been recorded yet.

Factory-seal check: For a fresh set like OP-17, insist on photos of the uncut Bandai security tape, both side seams and the product code. Release-week stock moves fast, so re-taped opened displays do circulate — added shrink alone is not factory-seal evidence.
