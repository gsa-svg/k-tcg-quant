Set notes — price context, reprints, factory-seal check, background

OP-13 box price, 2025 reprints and factory-seal checks

As of 2026-08-12, current eBay asks center near $215 across 5 verified listings. The separate tracked market series last recorded about $131 on 2026-07-12. OP-13 combines a Five Elders parallel, an Ace wanted-poster treatment and a separately tracked red manga Luffy. Variant matching matters here because standard and red manga listings cannot be combined into one chase-card price.

Reprint record: The source ledger records retailer reprint activity in September and November 2025. Those dated records show that additional retail availability occurred, but they do not disclose print volume or establish a future restock schedule.

Factory-seal check: Verify that the original Bandai security tape is uncut and that photos show the tape edges, side seams, bottom and product code. OP-13 loose packs, mixed-language lots and listings that hide the seal area are excluded from sealed-box comparisons.

The Gorosei era opens

Carrying on His Will is where the endgame cast arrives: the parallel-art St. Marcus Mars — one of the Five Elders — tops the chase table, a striking result for a brand-new antagonist. Behind him sit an Ace wanted-poster frame and the red manga-art Luffy, the scarcer red-stamped cousin of the standard manga class, which we track as its own variant.

Brand-new-character chases carry discovery risk in both directions: no price history, no population base, everything still being decided. That is what makes this late-2025 box one of the more interesting ones in our daily data — and why sold prices deserve even more weight over asking prices here than usual.
