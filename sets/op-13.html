Set notes — price context, reprints, factory-seal check, background

OP-13 box price, 2025 reprints and factory-seal checks

As of 2026-08-23, current eBay asks center near $215 across 10 verified listings. Completed eBay sales last put the box near $129 on 2026-08-23. OP-13 combines a Five Elders parallel, an Ace wanted-poster treatment and a separately tracked red manga Luffy. Variant matching matters here because standard and red manga listings cannot be combined into one chase-card price.

Reprint record: The source ledger records retailer reprint activity in September and November 2025. Those dated records show that additional retail availability occurred, but they do not disclose print volume or establish a future restock schedule.

Factory-seal check: Verify that the original Bandai security tape is uncut and that photos show the tape edges, side seams, bottom and product code. OP-13 loose packs, mixed-language lots and listings that hide the seal area are excluded from sealed-box comparisons.

The Gorosei era opens

Carrying on His Will is where the endgame cast arrives: the parallel-art St. Marcus Mars — one of the Five Elders — tops the chase table, a striking result for a brand-new antagonist. Behind him sit an Ace wanted-poster frame and the red manga-art Luffy, the scarcer red-stamped cousin of the standard manga class, which we track as its own variant.

Brand-new-character chases carry discovery risk in both directions: no price history, no population base, everything still being decided. That is what makes this late-2025 box one of the more interesting ones in our daily data — and why sold prices deserve even more weight over asking prices here than usual.

OP-13 box price: the tracked trajectory

The Japanese Carrying on His Will sealed box entered our tracking in May 2026 around $149 and stands near $129 as of 2026-08-23 — it fell 13% over the window, peaking at $158 in the week of 2026-07-19. The English-language Carrying on His Will box trades near $472 over the same period (+14% over its tracked window) — about 3.7x the Japanese box. This ratio does not explain why.

How the OP-13 box price compares with its tracked range (Aug 2026)

The box trades at $129, 18% under its tracked peak of $158 and down 13% over our tracking window. The top card alone (Monkey D. Luffy 118 Red Manga Alternate Art) is worth about 121x the box, so the visible chase value is highly concentrated. Official pull rates are unpublished. It is not opening expected value.

Reprints & original price

  • Original Japanese release: 2025-08-23 (Bandai official).
  • Original Japanese MSRP: ¥5,280 per 24-pack box (about $33).
  • Post-release lottery sale: Dec 2025 — a Premium Bandai / Bandai Namco shop draw held after release. Bandai does not call these reprints, so we do not either.
  • Retailer-reported restocks: Sep 2025, Nov 2025 — store restock notices, not a Bandai reprint announcement.

How fast is the OP-13 PSA population growing?

For Carrying on His Will, between 2025-12-03 and 2026-08-19, the recorded PSA population increased by 42,087 grades — peaking at 1,771 in the week of 2026-03-04, with the latest week at 1,113 (steady). The all-time set total is 46,928. Grades are not a box-opening count.
