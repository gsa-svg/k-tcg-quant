Set notes — price context, reprints, factory-seal check, background

OP-01 booster box price, reprint record and seal checks

As of 2026-08-23, current eBay asks center near $325 across 19 verified listings. Completed eBay sales last put the box near $305 on 2026-08-09. As the first main One Piece Card Game set, OP-01 attracts set-run collectors as well as buyers focused on its Luffy, Zoro and Law parallels. Compare the tracked market value with the current asking-price sample rather than treating one listing as the market.

Reprint record: No dated retailer or distributor reprint is currently recorded in our source ledger. That means no source-backed entry is on file; it is not proof that no additional OP-01 stock ever reached stores.

Factory-seal check: For an older Japanese OP-01 display, require clear photos of the original Bandai security tape in uncut condition, both side seams, the bottom and the product code. Added shrink alone is not factory-seal evidence.

The set everything else gets measured against

Romance Dawn is the One Piece Card Game's first main set, and its sealed boxes behave less like a current product and more like an early-era collectible: the original December 2022 print run has long since left retail, so secondary-market supply only shrinks. The chase side is led by the parallel-art Monkey D. Luffy 003 — the closest thing this game has to a flagship rookie card — with the parallel Zoro and Law from the same cycle right behind it.

Because it opens the numbering, OP-01 is also where collectors start when they work backwards through the run, which keeps steady demand on a box that stopped being printed years ago. We track it daily like every other set, but read its swings with that vintage lens: moves here are about scarcity, not pull-rate news.

OP-01 box price: the tracked trajectory

The Japanese Romance Dawn sealed box entered our tracking in Jun 2026 around $299 and stands near $305 as of 2026-08-09 — it held roughly flat (+2%) over the window, peaking at $324 in the week of 2026-08-02. The English-language Romance Dawn box trades near $1,459 over the same period (-18% over its tracked window) — about 4.8x the Japanese box. This ratio does not explain why.

How the OP-01 box price compares with its tracked range (Aug 2026)

The box trades at $305, 6% under its tracked peak of $324 and up 2% over our tracking window.
