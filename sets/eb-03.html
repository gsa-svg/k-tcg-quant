Set notes — price context, reprints, factory-seal check, background

EB-03 box price, reprint record and original Bandai tape

As of 2026-08-23, current eBay asks center near $130 across 12 verified listings. Completed eBay sales last put the box near $127 on 2026-08-21. EB-03 is driven by character collectors around Robin, Nami and Boa Hancock rather than by a conventional main-set chase mix. Because the tracking window is still young, dated sold and listing observations matter more than an isolated launch price.

Reprint record: No dated EB-03 reprint entry is currently present in the source ledger. The product is still relatively recent, so the absence of a record must not be read as evidence that its print cycle has ended.

Factory-seal check: For a Japanese EB-03 display, prioritize an uncut original Bandai security tape and photos of both side seams, the base and product code. Do not treat seller-added shrink as a substitute for the factory tape.

The all-heroines experiment

Heroine's Edition is the run's clearest demographic experiment: a box whose entire premium end is female characters — Nico Robin, Nami and Boa Hancock in SP treatment. No other product in the game concentrates that collector segment so completely.

Products aimed at character collectors rather than players sell through differently: steadier, less tied to tournament news, more sensitive to gift-season timing. It released in February 2026, so our ledger on it is still young — the auction data lower on this page is the least filtered view of how the experiment is landing.

EB-03 box price: the tracked trajectory

The Japanese Heroine's Edition sealed box entered our tracking in Jun 2026 around $120 and stands near $127 as of 2026-08-21 — it gained 6% over the window, peaking at $134 in the week of 2026-08-07. The English-language Heroine's Edition box trades near $328 over the same period (+9% over its tracked window) — about 2.6x the Japanese box. This ratio does not explain why.

How the EB-03 box price compares with its tracked range (Aug 2026)

The box trades at $127, 5% under its tracked peak of $134 and up 6% over our tracking window. The top card alone (Boa Hancock SP) is worth about 12x the box, so the visible chase value is highly concentrated. Official pull rates are unpublished. It is not opening expected value.
