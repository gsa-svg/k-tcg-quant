Set notes — price context, reprints, factory-seal check, background

The schemers' set

Kingdoms of Intrigue is the villains-and-plotters chapter of the run, and its top end reflects it: manga-art Sabo up front, with special-treatment SP versions of Boa Hancock and Trafalgar Law behind him. SP cards — stamped character favorites folded into later boxes — are how this game keeps older fan favorites circulating, and two of them anchor this set's premium tier.

It closed the third quarter of 2023, one set before the release that changed the game's economics entirely, so OP-04 tends to be read as the last quiet box of the early era. That framing matters when you compare its chart to what came next.

OP-04 box price: the six-month trajectory

The Japanese Kingdoms of Intrigue sealed box entered Jan 2026 around $82 and stands near $108 as of 2026-07-11 — it gained 32% over the window, peaking at $129 in the week of 2026-06-24. The English-language Kingdoms of Intrigue box trades near $506 over the same period (+25% since January) — about 4.7x the Japanese box. This ratio does not explain why.

How the OP-04 box price compares with its tracked range (Aug 2026)

The box trades at $119, 16% under its tracked peak of $142 and up 32% over our tracking window. The top card alone (Boa Hancock SP) is worth about 5.3x the box, so the visible chase value is highly concentrated. Official pull rates are unpublished. It is not opening expected value.
