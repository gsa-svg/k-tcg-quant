Set notes — price context, reprints, factory-seal check, background

All in on one character

A Fist of Divine Speed does something no earlier main set did so nakedly: its top three chases are all the same character. Gold-stamped SP Luffy, standard SP Luffy, manga-art Luffy — the entire premium end of the box is one man.

That makes OP-11 the purest single-character bet in the run. If Gear-5-era Luffy demand stays where it is, the box needs nothing else; if it fades, there is no second act in the chase list to catch it. We flag concentration like this because a box's risk profile lives in the shape of its table, not in its title.

OP-11 box price: the six-month trajectory

The Japanese A Fist of Divine Speed sealed box entered Jan 2026 around $70 and stands near $120 as of 2026-07-12 — it gained 71% over the window, peaking at $134 in the week of 2026-06-03. The English-language A Fist of Divine Speed box trades near $540 over the same period (+62% since January) — about 4.5x the Japanese box. This ratio does not explain why.

How the OP-11 box price compares with its tracked range (Aug 2026)

The box trades at $133, 10% under its tracked peak of $149 and up 71% over our tracking window. The top card alone (Monkey D. Luffy 119 SP Gold) is worth about 70x the box, so the visible chase value is highly concentrated. Official pull rates are unpublished. It is not opening expected value.
