Set notes — price context, reprints, factory-seal check, background

Law's set, Whitebeard's shadow

Royal Blood is the lineage set — family names and inherited wills — and its chase list leans on two permanent fan favorites: a manga-art Trafalgar Law 119 at the top and an Edward Newgate SP behind it, with a treasure-rare Ace keeping the Marineford thread alive.

Between this appearance and his earlier SP and treasure-rare printings, Law holds buyers across sets in a way few characters other than Luffy manage. Boxes from spring 2025 are recent enough that some retail supply still exists, so the sealed premium here runs thinner than on anything from 2023.

OP-10 box price: the tracked trajectory

The Japanese Royal Blood sealed box entered our tracking on 2026-05-27 around $100 and stands near $88 as of 2026-10-07 — it fell 12% over the window, peaking at $109 in the week of 2026-07-01. The English-language Royal Blood box trades near $254 as of 2026-10-12 (+12% over its tracked window) — about 2.9x the Japanese box (JP as of 2026-10-07, EN as of 2026-10-12). This ratio does not explain why.

How the OP-10 box price compares with its tracked range (2026-10-14)

The box trades at $88, 19% under its tracked peak of $109 and down 12% over our tracking window. The top card alone (Trafalgar Law 119 Manga) is worth about 5.7x the box, so the visible chase value is highly concentrated. Official pull rates are unpublished. It is not opening expected value.
