Set notes — price context, reprints, factory-seal check, background

The old guard takes the top slot

Two Legends is exactly what its name promises — a set built around the game's elder statesmen. The parallel manga-art Silvers Rayleigh leads it, one of the few times a mentor-generation character out-chases every protagonist in the box, with an Ace SP and a treasure-rare Luffy close behind.

Rayleigh chases age well because his premium card count across the whole run is small; there are only so many to collect. That, more than arc hype, is what gives this autumn-2024 box its particular floor — and it makes the reprint section below worth reading more carefully than usual.

OP-08 box price: the tracked trajectory

The Japanese Two Legends sealed box entered our tracking in Jun 2026 around $86 and stands near $79 as of 2026-08-24 — it fell 8% over the window, peaking at $86 in the week of 2026-06-29. The English-language Two Legends box trades near $250 as of 2026-08-25 (+11% over its tracked window) — about 3.2x the Japanese box (JP as of 2026-08-24, EN as of 2026-08-25). This ratio does not explain why.

How the OP-08 box price compares with its tracked range (Aug 2026)

The box trades at $79, 8% under its tracked peak of $86 and down 8% over our tracking window. The top card alone (Silvers Rayleigh Parallel Manga) is worth about 5.6x the box, so the visible chase value is highly concentrated. Official pull rates are unpublished. It is not opening expected value.
