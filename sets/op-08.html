Set notes — price context, reprints, factory-seal check, background

The old guard takes the top slot

Two Legends is exactly what its name promises — a set built around the game's elder statesmen. The parallel manga-art Silvers Rayleigh leads it, one of the few times a mentor-generation character out-chases every protagonist in the box, with an Ace SP and a treasure-rare Luffy close behind.

Rayleigh chases age well because his premium card count across the whole run is small; there are only so many to collect. That, more than arc hype, is what gives this autumn-2024 box its particular floor — and it makes the reprint section below worth reading more carefully than usual.

OP-08 box price: the tracked trajectory

The Japanese Two Legends sealed box entered our tracking on 2026-06-12 around $81 and stands near $80 as of 2026-10-09 — it held roughly flat (-1%) over the window, peaking at $86 in the week of 2026-08-14. The English-language Two Legends box trades near $258 as of 2026-10-14 (+15% over its tracked window) — about 3.2x the Japanese box (JP as of 2026-10-09, EN as of 2026-10-14). This ratio does not explain why.
