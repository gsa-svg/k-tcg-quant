Set notes — price context, reprints, factory-seal check, background

Egghead arrives, and Hancock rules it

500 Years in the Future is the Egghead-arc set, but its market identity belongs to Boa Hancock: her parallel manga-art 051 is the strongest female-character chase in the main-set run to that point, leading the table ahead of the O-Nami SP and a treasure-rare Law.

Sets whose top card is not a protagonist behave differently — demand comes from character collectors as much as players, and that base is loyal. Hancock's population growth at the graders is the number to watch if you hold this box sealed; the grading figures further down the page come straight from our ledger.

OP-07 box price: the tracked trajectory

The Japanese 500 Years in the Future sealed box entered our tracking in May 2026 around $87 and stands near $80 as of 2026-09-05 — it fell 8% over the window, peaking at $95 in the week of 2026-08-01. The English-language 500 Years in the Future box trades near $264 as of 2026-09-10 (-12% over its tracked window) — about 3.3x the Japanese box (JP as of 2026-09-05, EN as of 2026-09-10). This ratio does not explain why.

How the OP-07 box price compares with its tracked range (Sep 2026)

The box trades at $80, 16% under its tracked peak of $95 and down 8% over our tracking window. The top card alone (Boa Hancock 051 Parallel Manga) is worth about 14x the box, so the visible chase value is highly concentrated. Official pull rates are unpublished. It is not opening expected value.
