Egghead arrives, and Hancock rules it

500 Years in the Future is the Egghead-arc set, but its market identity belongs to Boa Hancock: her parallel manga-art 051 is the strongest female-character chase in the main-set run to that point, leading the table ahead of the O-Nami SP and a treasure-rare Law.

Sets whose top card is not a protagonist behave differently — demand comes from character collectors as much as players, and that base is loyal. Hancock's population growth at the graders is the number to watch if you hold this box sealed; the grading figures further down the page come straight from our ledger.

OP-07 box price: the six-month trajectory

The Japanese sealed box entered Jan 2026 around $51 and stands near $82 as of 2026-07-12 — it gained 61% over the window, peaking at $90 in the week of 2026-04-22. The English-language OP-07 box trades near $400 over the same period (+14% since January) — about 4.9x the Japanese box, a gap driven by print volume and Western demand rather than card content.

Is an OP-07 booster box worth buying? (Aug 2026)

The box sits near its tracked high ($82 vs peak $90, +61% over our window) — the market has rewarded holders, and a buyer today is paying for that momentum to continue. The chase math is lottery-shaped: the top card alone (Boa Hancock 051 Parallel Manga) is worth about 22x the box, so a box's expected value concentrates in a few low-odds hits.
